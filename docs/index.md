# Brain Workout — Hjernetrim

A collection of small brain-training puzzles for Android, built for comfortable
reading: large text, big tap targets, high contrast, and no timers.

Available in Norwegian (Bokmål) and English.

- [Privacy policy](privacy)
- [Source code](https://github.com/leifskje/brain-workout)

The app collects no data. Everything stays on your device.
