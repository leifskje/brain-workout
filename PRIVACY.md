# Privacy Policy — Brain Workout (Hjernetrim)

*Last updated: 11 June 2026*

Brain Workout is a collection of brain-training mini-games.

## Data collection

**This app does not collect, store, or share any personal data.**

- All game progress (levels, stars, streaks, settings) is stored **only on
  your device** and never leaves it.
- The app contains **no ads**, **no analytics**, **no tracking**, and **no
  user accounts**.
- The app makes **no network requests**. The only exception is the optional
  "Support the developer" link, which opens the developer's Ko-fi page in
  your web browser — nothing is sent by the app itself.

## Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored progress.
